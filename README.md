# Food Trade

Send food between your own cities in Civilization VI: Gathering Storm. Pick a sender, a receiver and an amount in the Food Trade panel. Routes start at the next turn and need no trader, road or other connection.

Food comes from the sender's own surplus. If that surplus drops, older routes get food first and later routes send what's left. Routes recover automatically when the surplus returns. Food received from another city can't be sent on again.

Each active route costs 1 gold per turn, plus 1 gold for every 4 food sent, rounded up. A route sending 3 food costs 2 gold. A route sending 10 costs 4 gold. A route sending no food still costs 1 gold. If you can't pay, the newest routes switch off until you can afford them again.

Open Food Trade from the launch bar. Create a route, edit its amount or cancel it in the panel. A route ends if either city is lost or it sends no food for 10 turns in a row. You'll get a notification when a route is created, reduced, restored or ended, and when gold runs short.

Requires Gathering Storm. AI players don't create routes. Single player and multiplayer are intended; in-game and multiplayer validation is still pending.

Install the `FDT` folder in your Civilization VI Mods folder, then enable Food Trade in Additional Content for a new game. The separate development mod is for testing and isn't needed to play.
